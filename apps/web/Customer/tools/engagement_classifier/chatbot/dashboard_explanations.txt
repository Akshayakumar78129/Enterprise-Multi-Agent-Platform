Customer Engagement Dashboard Documentation

OVERVIEW
The Customer Engagement Dashboard is a comprehensive analytics tool designed to help businesses understand and improve their customer relationships. It provides insights into customer behavior, engagement levels, and actionable recommendations for business growth.

KPI TILES
The dashboard features four main KPI (Key Performance Indicator) tiles:

1. Total Customers
- Displays the complete count of customers in your database
- Helps track business growth over time
- Includes both active and inactive customers
- Updated in real-time as new customers are added

2. Average Engagement Score
- Shows the mean engagement score across all customers
- Scores range from 1-10 (10 being highest engagement)
- Calculated using RFM analysis (Recency, Frequency, Monetary)
- Good benchmark: scores above 7 indicate healthy customer relationships

3. Average Days Since Activity
- Measures how recently customers have interacted with your business
- Lower numbers indicate more active customer base
- Helps identify if customers are becoming less engaged over time
- Benchmark: under 30 days average is excellent

4. Re-engagement Opportunities
- Count of customers who can potentially be won back
- Typically customers with medium-low engagement who haven't churned completely
- These represent your best ROI for marketing campaigns
- Focus area for retention strategies

ENGAGEMENT PYRAMID
The engagement pyramid is a visual representation of your customer base segmentation:

High Engagement Customers (Top of Pyramid)
- Engagement scores: 8-10
- Characteristics: Recent activity (within 30 days), frequent purchases, high transaction values
- Behavior: Respond well to marketing, likely to refer others, high lifetime value
- Strategy: Retain through VIP programs, exclusive offers, personalized service
- Typical percentage: 20-30% of customer base

Medium Engagement Customers (Middle of Pyramid)
- Engagement scores: 5-7
- Characteristics: Moderate activity (30-90 days), occasional purchases, average transaction values
- Behavior: Mixed response to marketing, potential for growth, price-sensitive
- Strategy: Targeted campaigns, product recommendations, limited-time offers
- Typical percentage: 40-50% of customer base

Low Engagement Customers (Base of Pyramid)
- Engagement scores: 1-4
- Characteristics: Inactive (90+ days), rare purchases, low transaction values
- Behavior: Poor response to marketing, at risk of churning, may have unresolved issues
- Strategy: Win-back campaigns, surveys, special discounts, direct outreach
- Typical percentage: 20-30% of customer base

CUSTOMER TABLE
The main customer table displays detailed information about each customer:

Columns Explained:
- Customer ID: Unique identifier for each customer
- Customer Name: Full name of the customer
- Engagement Score: Calculated score from 1-10 based on RFM analysis
- Engagement Level: Categorical classification (High/Medium/Low)
- Last Activity Date: Most recent interaction with your business
- Days Since Activity: Number of days since last interaction
- Total Transactions: Lifetime number of purchases
- Total Spent: Lifetime monetary value of customer
- Average Order Value: Mean transaction amount
- RFM Scores: Individual Recency, Frequency, and Monetary scores

ENGAGEMENT SCORING METHODOLOGY
The engagement scoring system uses RFM analysis:

Recency (R) - 40% weight:
- How recently did the customer make a purchase?
- Score 1-5: 5 = very recent (within 7 days), 1 = very old (over 365 days)
- Recent customers are more likely to purchase again

Frequency (F) - 30% weight:
- How often does the customer make purchases?
- Score 1-5: 5 = very frequent (weekly), 1 = very rare (once per year)
- Frequent customers show loyalty and engagement

Monetary (M) - 20% weight:
- How much does the customer spend?
- Score 1-5: 5 = high spender (top 20%), 1 = low spender (bottom 20%)
- High-value customers contribute more to revenue

Activity Type - 10% weight:
- Quality of customer interactions
- Considers email opens, website visits, support interactions
- Meaningful engagement beyond just purchases

BUSINESS INSIGHTS
Key Metrics to Monitor:
1. Engagement score trends over time
2. Customer distribution across engagement levels
3. Days since activity patterns
4. Re-engagement opportunity counts

Warning Signs:
- Decreasing average engagement scores
- Increasing days since activity

INSIGHTS & STRATEGIES

1. Reducing Inactive Customers
- Send targeted win-back campaigns (email + discounts).
- Offer time-limited promotions to spark urgency.
- Use surveys to discover reasons for inactivity and fix pain points.

2. Improving Engagement Scores
- Introduce loyalty programs with tiered rewards.
- Gamify engagement (badges, progress tracking, referral bonuses).
- Provide educational webinars or content to add value beyond transactions.

3. Retaining High-Value Customers
- Create exclusive VIP experiences (early access, dedicated support).
- Personalize offers based on past behavior and purchase history.
- Regularly request feedback and implement changes.

4. Strengthening Medium Engagement Segment
- Run personalized recommendation campaigns.
- Provide bundled offers or subscription models.
- Use retargeting ads and remarketing emails.

5. Preventing Customer Churn
- Monitor “at-risk” customers with low scores and long inactivity.
- Reach out proactively before they churn.
- Offer meaningful incentives (loyalty points, free upgrades).

6. Long-Term Growth Strategies
- Balance acquisition vs retention (avoid over-focusing on just new customers).
- Build predictive models to identify future churn risks.
- Regularly benchmark engagement metrics against industry standards.
