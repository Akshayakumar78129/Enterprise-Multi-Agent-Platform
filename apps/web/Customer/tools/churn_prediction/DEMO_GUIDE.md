## 🎯 Key Features to Explore

### 1. **Animated KPI Tiles** 🎪
- **What to see:** Four beautiful KPI cards with counting animations
- **Try this:** Watch the numbers count up from 0 when the page loads
- **Hover effect:** Move your mouse over each tile to see the glow effect
- **Icons:** Each KPI has a unique emoji icon that rotates on load

### 2. **Interactive Risk Pyramid** 📊
- **Location:** Top-left visualization
- **What to see:** Pyramid showing customer risk distribution
- **Try this:** Hover over each section to see detailed tooltips
- **Animation:** Watch the pyramid build from bottom to top

### 3. **Probability Histogram** 📈
- **Location:** Top-right visualization  
- **What to see:** Beautiful gradient bars showing churn probability distribution
- **Try this:** Hover over bars to see exact customer counts
- **Feature:** Notice the smooth gradient colors from green to red

### 4. **Feature Importance Chart** 🧠
- **Location:** Bottom-left visualization
- **What to see:** Horizontal bars showing which factors predict churn
- **Try this:** 
  - Click the dropdown to sort alphabetically vs by importance
  - Hover over bars to see detailed descriptions
  - Notice the custom icons for each feature

### 5. **Temporal Risk Patterns** ⏰
- **Location:** Bottom-right visualization
- **What to see:** Stacked area chart showing risk trends over time
- **Try this:**
  - Click the time range buttons (7d, 30d, 90d)
  - Hover over the chart to see daily breakdowns
  - Watch the smooth area transitions

### 6. **Enhanced Customer Table** 👥
- **Location:** Bottom section
- **What to see:** Clean, modern table with customer data
- **Try this:**
  - Click column headers to sort
  - Hover over rows to see highlight effects
  - Notice the improved risk badges and progress bars
